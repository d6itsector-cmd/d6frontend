# Site-wide photography

All photos in this folder are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and are used
under the [Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial
use, no attribution required. They were taken from Unsplash's own CDN
(`images.unsplash.com`, which serves only Unsplash-Licence photos; paid
Unsplash+ images are served from `plus.unsplash.com` and none are used here),
cropped and converted to WebP by that CDN, and stored locally.

Every photo was reviewed before use. Photos with visible third-party brand
logos (laptop makers, coffee chains, sportswear, phone brands, payment
terminals) were rejected so nothing implies an endorsement of D6 Global
Services. These are illustrative stock photos of real people and places; none is
presented as a D6 client project.

Service and Custom Products photography is listed separately in
`src/assets/services/CREDITS.md`.
